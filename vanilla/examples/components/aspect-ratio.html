    Aspect Ratio

    Aspect Ratio

    Displays content within a desired ratio.

    16:9 (Widescreen)

    Common video aspect ratio for YouTube, streaming, and modern displays. 

    <div class="aspect-ratio aspect-ratio-16-9">
  <img src="..." alt="Image">
</div>

    4:3 (Standard)

    Classic photo and display aspect ratio. 

    <div class="aspect-ratio aspect-ratio-4-3">
  <img src="..." alt="Image">
</div>

    1:1 (Square)

    Perfect square, commonly used for profile pictures and thumbnails.

    1:1
    <div class="aspect-ratio aspect-ratio-1-1">
  <img src="..." alt="Image">
</div>
